# Soundtrack measurements

Generated by `tools/audio/music/build.sh` (do not edit). Every number is measured on the
encoded file after decoding it again with ffmpeg, not on the master.

- **LUFS**: integrated loudness (Loudness Units relative to Full Scale, EBU R128, measured with
  ffmpeg `ebur128`). **LRA**: loudness range in LU.
- **true peak**: highest inter-sample peak in decibels true peak (dBTP). 0 would clip.
- **length error**: decoded length minus the length the score calls for.
- Loop seam, for loops only: **repeat** is how closely the 200 ms around the loop end repeat the
  200 ms around the loop start (dB below the signal; lower is closer). It cannot be better than
  about the **codec** column plus 3 dB, which is the encoder's own error in those windows.
  **step** is the sample step across the splice divided by the 99th percentile of the steps
  within 50 ms of it (a click would be far above 1). **flux** is the onset energy at the splice
  relative to the same moment played straight through into the pad (0 dB: the jump adds nothing).
- `*_sum` rows are the base and combat stems of a stage decoded and added at full level.
